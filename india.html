India

Discover Kerala's lush landscapes, tranquil backwaters, tea gardens and rich cultural experiences.

Explore places to visit and areas to stay. These hotel-area suggestions can help you choose accommodation that suits your itinerary.

Places to visit

  • Alappuzha — houseboat stays on the backwaters
  • Munnar — tea gardens and rolling hill scenery
  • Kochi — Fort Kochi, Chinese fishing nets and colonial architecture
  • Thekkady — Periyar and its surrounding natural landscapes

Where to stay

  • Fort Kochi — explore the historic quarter and cultural attractions
  • Munnar town or Chithirapuram — access to tea gardens and hill scenery
  • Alappuzha — stay by the backwaters or near the houseboat jetties
  • Kumily — a convenient base for visiting Thekkady and Periyar

Kerala travel tips

  • Plan travel between Kochi, Munnar, Thekkady and Alappuzha in daylight; winding hill roads can take longer than expected.
  • For houseboat stays, confirm what meals, transfers and overnight facilities are included before booking.
  • Bring light rain protection and insect repellent, especially during humid or rainy periods.
  • Choose licensed guides and registered taxis, and keep some cash for smaller shops and local transport.
