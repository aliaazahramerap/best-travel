Nepal

Discover Kathmandu's heritage and Himalayan scenery on a journey that brings culture and nature together.

Explore places to visit and areas to stay. These hotel-area suggestions can help you choose accommodation that suits your itinerary.

Places to visit

  • Kathmandu Durbar Square — a historic palace square and temples
  • Swayambhunath — a hilltop stupa overlooking the valley
  • Boudhanath — one of Nepal's largest stupas
  • Patan Durbar Square — traditional architecture and craftsmanship

Where to stay

  • Thamel — a wide choice of restaurants, shops and tour operators
  • Boudha — stay near the stupa in a quieter setting
  • Patan — convenient for architecture, museums and historic squares
  • Lazimpat — hotels and restaurants north of the city centre

Nepal travel tips

  • Build flexibility into your schedule: road journeys around Kathmandu can take longer due to traffic or changing conditions.
  • Carry small-denomination Nepalese rupees for local purchases, and check which payment methods are accepted before heading out.
  • Wear comfortable walking shoes for uneven lanes, steps and temple courtyards.
  • Ask before photographing people or ceremonies, and follow posted visitor guidance at temples and stupas.
