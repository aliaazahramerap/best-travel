Turkey

Discover a remarkable blend of history, culture and scenery, from Istanbul to Cappadocia.

Explore places to visit and areas to stay. These hotel-area suggestions can help you choose accommodation that suits your itinerary.

Places to visit

  • Istanbul — Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque and the Grand Bazaar
  • Cappadocia — hot-air balloon experiences and rock valleys
  • Pamukkale — travertine terraces and the ancient city of Hierapolis
  • Antalya — the old town of Kaleiçi and Mediterranean beaches

Where to stay

  • Sultanahmet, Istanbul — close to major historic landmarks
  • Karaköy or Galata, Istanbul — cafés, restaurants and tram access
  • Göreme, Cappadocia — convenient for valleys and local tours
  • Kaş or Kaleiçi, Antalya — choose between a coastal or historic-town setting

Turkey travel tips

  • Allow extra time for Istanbul traffic and use contactless transit cards or official ticket machines for public transport.
  • Book Cappadocia balloon flights with licensed operators, and keep a spare morning in your schedule in case weather causes cancellations.
  • Carry a light layer for evenings and early starts, especially in Cappadocia where temperatures can vary.
  • At mosques, dress respectfully and check visitor hours; prayer times may affect access to some areas.
