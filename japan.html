Japan

Discover Kyoto's timeless temples, peaceful gardens and atmospheric historic streets.

Explore places to visit and areas to stay. These accommodation-area suggestions can help you choose a base that suits your itinerary.

Places to visit

  • Higashiyama, Kyoto — preserved lanes, temples and traditional streets
  • Fushimi Inari Taisha — mountain trails lined with red torii gates
  • Arashiyama — bamboo grove, riverside scenery and Tenryu-ji
  • Nishiki Market — local food stalls and Kyoto ingredients

Where to stay

  • Downtown Kyoto — convenient for restaurants, shops and transit
  • Higashiyama — close to historic temples and traditional streets
  • Kyoto Station area — useful for rail connections and day trips
  • Arashiyama — a quieter base near the river and western Kyoto sights

Japan travel tips

  • Reserve seats on popular long-distance trains in advance, especially during busy travel seasons.
  • Use a rechargeable IC transit card where supported, and check local bus and train schedules before day trips.
  • Carry cash for smaller businesses and keep a small bag for rubbish, as public bins can be limited.
  • Follow local etiquette at temples and shrines, and check photography rules before taking pictures.
